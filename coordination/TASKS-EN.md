- [ ] Verify local execution, share read/write, actual hardware/browser/tools and current competition requirements. Preserve previous projects.
- [ ] Lock one original concept and its signature interaction. One-screen concept description; no routine approval needed.
- [ ] Build a representative true-3D slice: authored cyberpunk identity, layered spatial detail, a close camera move and meaningful user feedback.
- [ ] Test the renderer, quality modes and performance budget. Complete a short native 3840x2160/120fps capture/encode/decode check before full recording.
- [ ] Complete the coherent website world, interactions, lighting, material detail and selected genuine Lux3D assets. Report procedural and generated contributions accurately.
- [ ] Validate permitted camera paths, close details, interaction/reset flow, loading/error behavior and actual tested runtime performance.
- [ ] Export the native-4K120 master and representative 4K stills; label any compatibility derivative separately. Verify dimensions, frame count/rate, duration and playback.
- [ ] Produce and visually check the PDF report. Package editable sources, assets, reproducible commands, provenance and dependency/license information.
- [ ] Verify the final shared-location build. Complete the Chinese title/introduction, submission map and concise test/limitation record. Prepare publication materials; do not submit forms or accept terms as the user.

A blocked target stays explicitly UNMET, with evidence and the best valid outputs preserved. A video specification is not a real-time benchmark. Finished product quality takes priority over adding more features.
